PRESSURIZED

A Tactile Arcade Experience by Holden Denyer

PRESSURIZED is the capstone project of Holden Denyer, acting as a culmination of his work in interactive design and immersive experiences. Control a submarine on a deep sea mission while managing the many, many complex inputs that keep the vessel running. Try to manage oncoming enemies, a failing oxygen system, and a rapidly decreasing pressure level as you try to survive the depths of the ocean.



About the artist

My name is Holden Denyer. I am an artist and designer working at the intersection of interactive systems, physical media, and immersive digital environments. My work explores how people engage with technology through touch, motion, and spatial interaction.

Drawing from game design, installation art, and experimental interfaces, my projects often blend software and hardware to create experiences that feel playful, tactile, and responsive. I am particularly interested in emergent interactions.

I am passionate about Graphic Design and building immersive experiences for users in roles like Exhibit Designer, Creative Director, and Interactive Designer.
