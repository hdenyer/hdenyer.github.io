Lerp Heart

2024 • C# in Unity

An unsettling animation utilizing linear interpolation interfaces.

Overview

Lerp Heart is an experimental Unity project utilizing Linear Interpretation interfaces. On start, the heart is created with many individual spheres, following coordinates determined by a cartesian formula. The Lerp() command is then used to transform the scale of each sphere in succession, establishing an effect that appears as liquid pumping through the shape. Color is also altered in this loop, highlighting the scaled up areas with crimson red and furthering the blood-pumping motif and making the classic heart shape all too literal.
