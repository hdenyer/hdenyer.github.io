Artist Statement

Holden Denyer is an Immersive Media Design student at the University of
Maryland.

Through

his

studies,

he

gained

an

immense

appreciation

for

space-making

and

creating

a

sense

of

ﬂow.

His

work

is

deﬁned

by

distinct

edges

which

outline

a

simpliﬁcation

of

the

world

around

him;

giving

things

rigidity,

organizing

them

so

that

they

feel

balanced.

Whether on a digital canvas or a real-world switch board, Holden strives to
create

art

that

involves

its

audience,

interactive

by

visual

mechanics.

Many

of

his

works

utilize

a

doodle-like

style

to

encapsulate

the

real

world

and

hyperfocus

on

the

nuances

of

everyday

life,

formatting

them

for

scientiﬁc

discovery

or

a

reconsideration

of

the

ﬂow

of

it

all.

He

continues

to

enhance

his

work

and

deﬁne

his

personal

style

as

he

completes

his

studies

and

prepares

to

enter

the

world

of

design.
