Required Hardware - PRESSURIZED arcade cabinet (plywood, self-contained unit) - PRESSURIZED control deck (mounted inside cabinet lower section) - Monitor / display (mounted inside cabinet upper section) - MacBook or compatible computer running the Unity build - Bluetooth speaker / boombox (for audio output, connected to Mac) - Arduino Mega x2, Arduino Uno x1 (pre-installed in controller) - USB cables: one per Arduino (3 total), routed from controller to laptop - Power strip (for laptop, monitor, LED strips, and alarm light) - LED strip power supply (pre-installed in controller) - Alarm siren light (mounted on cabinet exterior, pre-wired)
