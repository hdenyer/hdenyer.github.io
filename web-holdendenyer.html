Overview

holdendenyer.com represents an early version of this website, originally built in 2022. It was one of my first personal HTML projects and served as the starting point for the gallery style that still defines my portfolio today. While far from perfect — its responsiveness was limited and its content minimal — it featured a simple JavaScript menu and showcased 15 works. Looking back, this project highlights both the clarity of direction I had early on and the web design experience I had yet to gain. It stands as an important milestone that makes the current site’s sophistication feel all the more meaningful.
