Photography is an occasional extension of my visual practice, used to document and reinterpret the way I frame the world. I focus on aligning edges, parallels, and balance within a scene, and often experiment with film to embrace grain, softness, and imperfect vision. My photographic work commonly incorporates color manipulation and digital reformatting to better reflect the mood or perspective I intended for the shot.
