Something Fishy

2023 • Premiere Pro, ProCreate

A hand-drawn short film about a desperate village and a strange fish.

Overview

Something Fishy is an animated short film built entirely from transformed 2D artwork, created in ProCreate and animated through layered manipulation in Adobe Premiere Pro. Every scene is constructed from organized visual elements, allowing characters and environments to move, evolve, and interact in unusual ways. The film tells an absurd tale of a starving seaside village that discovers an enormous, mysterious fish— leading to a celebratory feast, an unsettling twist the following morning, and a strangely hopeful conclusion.
