TouchDesigner is a core tool in my visual and interaction practice, enabling real-time reactive systems, generative motion, and procedural effects. I use it to treat digital media as a dynamic canvas- one where visuals, audio, and physical inputs shape the outcome. My work explores responsive interfaces that blur this boundary between media and interaction.
