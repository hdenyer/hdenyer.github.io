A collection of professional portraits for university graduation.

Overview

I was commissioned by a group of graduating students to photograph their senior portraits across campus, giving me valuable experience in professional portraiture and event-style photography. The session included a variety of locations and lighting conditions, as well as creative direction tailored to each student. One student graduated with honors in entomology, inspiring several unique shots featuring insects as a thematic touch. All portrait subjects provided permission to be featured on this site.
