Traitors and Patriots

2024 • InDesign, Photoshop

A political zine featuring full-page collage spreads.

Overview

Traitors and Patriots is a political commentary zine created in Adobe InDesign that explores the blurred boundary between patriotism and traitorship. Featuring four illustrated articles and thematic collage spreads, the project blends thoughtful analysis with handmade visuals. Layout design, typography, and editorial pacing are supported by full-page collages developed in Photoshop, with hand-drawn illustrations from ProCreate. The result is a publication that pairs strong design organization with a uniquely expressive visual style.
