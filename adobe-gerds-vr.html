Gerd's VR

2022 • InDesign, ProCreate

A children's book discussing the potential for VR.

Overview

Gerd’s VR is a children’s book designed in Adobe InDesign featuring hand-drawn artwork created in ProCreate. The story follows Gerd - a simple, friendly character - as he explains the basic functionality and potential of VR technology. Designed for young readers, the visuals are colorful and approachable, and the writing is succinct and encouraging. This project demonstrates a careful balance of word choice, visual style, and audience consideration, bringing complex concepts into an accessible, playful format.
